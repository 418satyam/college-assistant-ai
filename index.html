About College Assistant AI

College Assistant AI is a conversational assistant built on Amazon Lex V2, designed to help students find information about courses, admissions, and contacts quickly and naturally.

  • Secured with temporary Amazon Cognito credentials
  • Supports voice input and spoken replies
  • Keeps your recent conversations on this device
  • Fast, static, and hosted entirely on GitHub Pages
